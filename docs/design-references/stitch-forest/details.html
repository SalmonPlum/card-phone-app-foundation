Lantern

Torch of the mindful pilgrim, illuminating not the whole world's darkness, but faithfully lighting the ground beneath your feet.

MEANING

Clear awareness focused on immediate surroundings. Light in the darkness revealing the next single step on the path.

This card invites you to return to the present moment. You do not need to know the destination or pierce the fog; simply trust each step as it arrives.
